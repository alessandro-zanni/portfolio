## The brief

A youth football academy ran player scouting on paper and scattered
spreadsheets. Observers filled in evaluation sheets from the sidelines;
staff needed to search, compare, and rank the players being watched from
one place, not from whoever had the latest file.

## What it does

- Mobile-first observation forms: scouts score players across eight common
  axes plus role-specific detail, straight from the field
- Player profiles with a radar chart built from weighted scores across
  every report, and cross-role score composition when a player has been
  watched in more than one position
- Ranking always scoped to a role and category — never an absolute list —
  and side-by-side comparison of up to three players
- Duplicate detection on player creation, admin tools for merging records,
  and time-boxed observer weights so a scout's track record feeds the
  ranking

![Player radar view, staging environment with demo data](/projects/scouty/radar.jpg)

## How it's built

Next.js 16 App Router with TypeScript, Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui on
`@base-ui/react` for the interface. Supabase backs a multi-tenant Postgres
schema secured with row-level security, with roles separating scouts from
managers and admins. Deployed to Vercel with separate production and
staging environments through GitHub Actions.

## Status

In production use with a real academy, including a one-time import of
their historical paper archive into the same schema. Still under active
development.

The project handles a real club's data, including minors, so the
repository and any live URL stay off the public portfolio.
