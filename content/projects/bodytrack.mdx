## The problem

A personal coach and their clients need one shared log for a training
journey — weight, measurements, meals, workouts — instead of numbers and
photos scattered across chat messages.

## What I built

- A daily log the client fills in: weight, measurements, progress photos,
  meals, workouts and adherence
- A coach view with trend charts over time and side-by-side photo comparison
- Invite-only access: the coach brings clients in, no self-signup
- Installable as a PWA, so it opens like any other app from a phone home
  screen, no app store required

## How it's built

Next.js 16 with TypeScript, Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui, Supabase in the EU
with row-level security tested by SQL assertions, deployed on Vercel with
separate production and staging environments.

## Outcome

Live at [bodytrack.it](https://bodytrack.it), used by the coach and their
clients, and still under active development on their requests.
