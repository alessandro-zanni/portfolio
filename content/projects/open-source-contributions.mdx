## The problem

Two open-source apps I use every day had gaps on my setup: a terminal music
player that froze on macOS audio edge cases, and a Jellyfin client that ran
on the Mac only as an iPad app.

## What I built

- **[spotatui](https://github.com/LargeModGames/spotatui)**, a terminal music
  player in Rust:
  [#498](https://github.com/LargeModGames/spotatui/pull/498) fixes two
  playback freezes and enables the alternative audio sources on macOS;
  [#499](https://github.com/LargeModGames/spotatui/pull/499) scans nested
  folders so a two-level local music library is browsable. Both merged
- **[Streamyfin](https://github.com/streamyfin/streamyfin)**, a Jellyfin
  client in Expo and React Native: a native Mac Catalyst build, proposed in
  [streamyfin#2080](https://github.com/streamyfin/streamyfin/issues/2080),
  with the player's Catalyst slice sent as
  [MPVKit#2](https://github.com/streamyfin/MPVKit/pull/2)

## How it's built

Each change follows the upstream project's conventions and review process,
not mine: issue first where the maintainers ask for one, small focused PRs,
forks kept aligned with upstream.

## Outcome

Two PRs merged into spotatui and shipped in its releases. The Streamyfin
Catalyst build is waiting on the maintainers.
