## The brief

A personal coach and their client need one shared log for a training
journey — workouts, meals, notes — instead of scattered messages across
chat apps.

## What it does

A daily diary the client fills in and the coach follows, installable as a
PWA so it opens like any other app on a phone home screen, no app-store
install required.

## Status

In active development, live at [bodytrack.it](https://bodytrack.it).
