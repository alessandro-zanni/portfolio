## The work

Corporate and portfolio websites for B2B and B2C clients, built and shipped
solo — brief, design, build, deploy, and the occasional late-night DNS fix.
Each site is a direct client relationship rather than agency work: scope,
timeline, and revisions handled end to end.

## What's consistent across them

- Responsive by default, tuned for the mobile-first traffic each client
  actually gets
- SEO groundwork — semantic markup, metadata, sitemaps — done at build time,
  not bolted on after launch
- Custom integrations where the brief calls for one: booking forms, CMS-backed
  content, third-party APIs

## How it's built

The toolset follows the client, not a house style: plain HTML/CSS/JS for
simple brochure sites, Astro or Next.js where content or interactivity
