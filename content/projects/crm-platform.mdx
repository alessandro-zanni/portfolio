## The brief

A distributor of electrical materials ran offers, quotations, and client
follow-up out of spreadsheets and phone calls. The sales team needed one
place to build an offer, track its status, and see a client's history
without waiting on someone else to update a file.

## What it does

- Client and offer management: create a quotation, attach line items, track
  status from draft to sent to accepted
- Authentication and per-user permissions, so the sales team works
  concurrently without stepping on each other's changes
- Real-time data sync — an offer updated by one user is current for everyone
  else immediately, no refresh required

## How it's built

Next.js on the frontend, a Node.js API, and Supabase/PostgreSQL for the data
layer and realtime subscriptions. Deployed to Vercel with zero-downtime
releases, so a new version ships without interrupting the sales team mid-day.
