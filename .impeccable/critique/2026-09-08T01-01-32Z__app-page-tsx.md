## Design Specificity Verdict

**Category-interchangeable, with a thin persona skin.** Swap the name in `lib/resume.ts` and this page serves any mid-level full-stack developer unchanged.

**LLM assessment:** The "terminal" concept is a skin, not load-bearing:
- **No color signature.** The entire palette is `oklch(L 0 0)` — pure greyscale in both themes. No prompt green/amber, no accent. `text-primary` is just darker grey. A terminal treatment with zero terminal colour is a mono font plus punctuation.
- **It hides the information architecture.** Real section headings (`Experience`, `Education`) are `sr-only`; the visible label is a faux command in 12px muted grey.
- **It dramatizes neither must-survive signal.** Production conversational-AI work and end-to-end ownership are two bullets among ~30 identically-weighted bullets.
- **It contradicts the rarest asset on the CV.** The pitch is "engineer with a formal design background." Nothing on this page could only have been made by someone with design training — it shows restraint, not craft. The obvious person-specific move (a *conversation* motif mirroring the chatbot/LLM work he ships) was available and skipped for the most generic indie-dev trope of 2020–2023.

Content and voice are authored for Alessandro. The design is not.

**Deterministic scan:** The bundled CLI detector (`detect.mjs` over `app/` + `components/`) is **clean — 0 findings.** The runtime overlay detector (`detect.js`, injected into the live page) found **13 findings across 5 rules**:

| Rule | Count | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `overused-font` | 1 | 100% of text is Geist Mono (`html { font-mono }`, `globals.css`) — agrees with the review's "monospace body is a readability tax" |
| `line-length` | 5 | ~99 chars/line in Experience/Projects/Education body paragraphs (aim <80); `max-w-3xl` + mono |
| `nested-cards` | 5 | Each TerminalBlock titlebar is a bordered/filled box inside the section's bordered `bg-card` box — **soft false positive** (deliberate terminal chrome) but structurally card-in-card |

Console on load: **clean** — no errors, no hydration warnings, no 404s, 20/20 requests `200`. ## Overall Impression

The **content and voice are right** — evidence-first, no hype, single source of truth in `lib/resume.ts` — and the **engineering is clean** (semantic structure, correct ARIA, instant static load). But the design is a competent template, not a work sample. For someone selling *design judgment + production AI depth*, the page proves neither: it's monochrome, one type family, decorative chrome with no information in it, and the two reasons to hire him are buried in an undifferentiated bullet wall. The single biggest opportunity: **make the first viewport state the positioning in plain English and make the page itself demonstrate the craft the CV claims.**

## What's Working

1. **Evidence-first copy in the right voice.** Every line traces to a role, a shipped product, or a named technology. No "passionate about," no superlatives. Exactly what the technical-peer audience needs, executed with discipline.
2. **Lean, correct, semantic engineering.** One `<h1>`, `header`/`nav`/`main`/`footer` landmarks, 5 `sr-only` `<h2>`s correctly wired via `aria-labelledby`, `mailto:`/`tel:` links, `aria-hidden` on decorative glyphs, zero console noise, static export. On the pure-engineering axis, "the page is the proof" partly holds.
3. **Calm spatial rhythm.** `max-w-3xl`, consistent `gap-10` between sections, generous card padding. The restraint is real.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Section identity is hidden behind faux-commands and jargon nav
- **What:** Visible section labels are `$ ls -la experience/`, `$ cat education.txt` in 12px muted grey; the real headings are `sr-only`. Nav reads `./experience ./projects ./skills ./contact`.
- **Why it matters:** The non-technical agency screener is an explicit primary audience and skims for the literal words "Experience / Education / Skills." They're not on the page as anything a sighted skimmer catches in 10 seconds. Breaks Product Principle 1 (skimmable in 10s for the screener *and* the peer).
- **Fix:** Promote real plain-English section titles to visible, prominent headings. Keep the command string only as a small caption above the title, or cut it. Nav labels → "Experience / Projects / Skills / Contact."
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The two must-survive signals are never composed for
- **What:** "Production LLM / conversational-AI work" and "end-to-end ownership (Postgres → self-hosted infra)" appear only as individual bullets inside the Heres list, at the same weight as "Client Requirements Analysis" three roles down.
- **Why it matters:** Product Principle 3 — these are *the* reasons to contact him rather than the next engineer, and must survive any redesign. They don't survive the current one.
- **Fix:** Add a short scannable positioning line in/under the hero naming both explicitly. Visually elevate the Heres role — lead with the role not the company, surface 2 bullets as highlights.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`

### [P1] The terminal concept is generic, slightly dated, and undercuts the design claim
- **What:** Monochrome traffic-light dots (zero information), two forever-blinking carets, `$` prompts, command-string headings — decoration with cognitive cost and no payload. No accent colour, one type family. Detector agrees (blinking-cursor, overused-font, nested-cards).
- **Why it matters:** Product Principle 4 — the page is a work sample for someone selling design judgment plus AI depth. A design-literate peer finds nothing here that demonstrates either.
- **Fix:** Either commit the metaphor to something only this person would build — a conversation / message motif mirroring his actual work, with deliberate typography and one accent colour — or drop it for a confident editorial layout that actually shows type, space and colour decisions. Do not split the difference.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape` (concept), then `/impeccable typeset` + `/impeccable colorize`

### [P2] Monospace body copy + undifferentiated bullet walls
- **What:** `html { font-mono }` sets Geist Mono for all long-form text (Inter is loaded but used for one paragraph). Heres has 7 two-line bullets, no chunking. The 2017–2018 trainee graphic-designer role ("cutting, covering, assembly") gets equal weight to the flagship AI role. Detector: `line-length` ×5 at ~99 chars, `overused-font`.
- **Why it matters:** Mono is a readability tax on paragraphs; the wall is the emotional valley; the trainee role dilutes the seniority signal.
- **Fix:** Body/bullet copy in Inter; reserve mono for labels, prompts, tags. Trim Heres to 4–5 highlights. Compress the trainee role to a one-liner or a condensed "earlier" list.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset` (+ `/impeccable distill`)

### [P2] Accessibility gaps for motion- and keyboard-dependent users
- **What:** Two `animate-caret` elements blink infinitely with no `prefers-reduced-motion` guard (WCAG 2.2.2). Hero links and the CV `<a download>` have only `hover:` styling — no explicit `focus-visible` ring (the well-configured `Button` component exists but is unused). `text-muted-foreground/60` separators fail AA contrast (2.30:1 light). Email — the most-wanted contact action — is the lowest-weight element in the CTA row.
- **Fix:** Gate the caret behind `motion-safe:` / a reduced-motion stop. Add explicit `focus-visible` rings to every link and the CV control. Lift the muted-separator opacity to clear AA. Make email a primary action, level with or above "download CV."
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` (+ `/impeccable polish`)

### [P3] Placeholder data ships as live, breakable links
- **What:** `GITHUB_URL = "https://github.com/alessandrozanni"` (flagged TODO in code) renders as a real link that likely 404s; `metadataBase` is also a placeholder (`https://alessandrozanni.dev`).
- **Fix:** Confirm the real URL or remove the GitHub link until it exists; set a real `metadataBase` before deploy.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (non-technical agency screener) — primary action: lift a clean title/years/stack summary and decide to forward.**
Lands on a black screen: `~/alessandro $ whoami`. No word "CV" or "Résumé" above the fold. Scrolls for "Experience" → finds `$ ls -la experience/`. Skills block reads `languages_frameworks: JavaScript/TypeScript, React, …` — machine formatting she must re-parse. Company names carry `s.r.l. / s.c.s. / s.n.c.` suffixes that look like noise. Current title and years exist only inside a 75-word pitch paragraph. **Likely outcome: mis-file or bounce.**

**Casey (distracted mobile user) — primary action: get in touch quickly.**
First screen (inferred from code): nav (4 links wrapping) + `whoami` + large name + blinking cursor + `title · subtitle` (wraps on the middot) + `# Barcelona` + 75-word pitch — all before any action. CTA row then wraps: "download CV" button, then "email / linkedin / github" drop to a second line at ~12px. Blinking caret competes with the CTA. To reach Contact he scrolls the whole 5-job / ~30-bullet mono column or taps `./contact`, which doesn't read as "get in touch." Email is a muted-grey 12px word. **Likely outcome: leaves without acting.**

**Sam (accessibility-dependent).**
SR structure is genuinely good — real `h1/h2/h3`, labelled sections, `aria-hidden` glyphs. But: two carets blink forever with no reduced-motion respect; keyboard focus indicators on hero links and the CV control are UA-default and hard to see on near-black; `$` prompt separators are announced literally ("dollar"); 12px muted-grey command text and `/60` separators are below comfortable low-vision contrast; first paint forces dark mode regardless of OS setting.

## Minor Observations

- **Name disappears on scroll** — sticky nav brands as `azanni@portfolio`; "Alessandro Zanni" is nowhere in the persistent header.
- **Forced dark mode** — `defaultTheme="dark"` with a `"dark"` (not `"system"`) fallback; first-time visitors always get dark regardless of `prefers-color-scheme`.
- **Auto-slugified tags read as broken** — `#various-backend-frameworks`, `#rest-apis` — prose run through `.replace(/\s+/g,"-")`.
- **Traffic-light dots are monochrome grey** — a recognizable shape stripped of its only information.
- **Freelance-client audience is invisible** — Contact copy says "Open to full-stack and AI engineering roles" only; prospective freelance clients are a target audience the page never addresses.
- **Light theme is nearly borderless** — `border: oklch(0.922)` on white; the terminal-pane cards almost float. The conceit weakens badly in light mode.
- **Nav has no active-section state; Education has no nav link.**
- **Footer** ends on "built with Next.js" — the last thing the visitor reads; make it a deliberate craft note or cut it.
- `new Date().getFullYear()` in a server component freezes at build time on static export.
- `scroll-mt-20` (80px) vs the ~49px sticky header — anchor landings leave ~30px dead space.

## Questions to Consider

1. Alessandro builds conversational AI for a living. Why is the page a *command line* and not a *conversation*? What would the structure look like if it were itself a sample of his actual specialty?
2. The formal design education is the rarest line on the CV. Point to one thing on this page that could **only** have been made by someone with design training. If you can't, what is the terminal skin proving?
3. If you deleted every piece of terminal chrome — dots, `$` prompts, both carets, the command strings — what is lost besides decoration, and how much skim speed is gained?
4. "One page, four audiences, no mode switch" — does that mean one flat neutral voice, or one page whose first viewport lets each reader self-select a path? Right now the freelance-client reader has no path at all.
